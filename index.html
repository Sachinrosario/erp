Installment plans
How installments work
Create installment plans per fee component. Each plan splits the fee amount into multiple due dates.

For example, Tuition Fee of ₹45,000 can be split:
Instalment 1 — ₹20,000 due 15 Jun
Instalment 2 — ₹15,000 due 15 Oct
Instalment 3 — ₹10,000 due 15 Jan

Students can pay each instalment separately. Partial payment and late fine tracking works per instalment.
